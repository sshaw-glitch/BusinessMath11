Future Value of Annuities

You get one try on each box. The page tells you if you are right or wrong, then you move on.

Your answers are sent to your teacher after every box.

How to type your answers

Dollar amounts: type the number, like 1234.56. Don't round until the very last step, then round to the nearest cent.

Functions: use * to multiply, / to divide, ^ for powers, and x for the variable. For example, 50*(1+0.02/12)^x (this is only an example, not an answer). Keep rates as fractions like 0.02/12 instead of rounding them.

Reading a graph: find where the curve crosses the dashed red line. A close estimate counts as right.
